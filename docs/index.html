Everything in one binder

Built for collectors, not spreadsheets.

From your first Foxparks to the last chase card, SphereDex keeps the whole set organised, valued, and easy to complete.

Every card, every set

All 255 cards across the Dawn of Palpagos booster, both trial decks, and the promos. Browse by set with real set art, track base cards and their variants, and watch your completion climb.

Real market values

See what your cards are actually worth: the average of the last 5 eBay sold prices, plus current live listings, in your own currency. Backed by SoldComps and Palworld Prices.

Biggest movers

The day's biggest risers and fallers across your collection or the whole set, so you always know what is heating up and what is cooling off.

Sealed products

Track booster boxes, packs, and trial decks, follow pre-orders for upcoming sets, and jump straight to buy links from eBay, Amazon, and trusted regional stockists.

Wishlist, graded & more

Keep a wishlist, mark favourites, log graded slabs (PSA, BGS, CGC and more), add notes, and run multiple collections. Import a CSV to get started in seconds.

Power up your spheres

Completion is a game. Fill sets, chase parallels, and climb the sphere ranks as your binder grows toward 100 percent.

Prices you can trust

Know what it is worth, before you buy or trade.

Open any card for the real numbers: the average of its last five sold prices, the recent sales behind that figure with links to each listing, and the current live asks. Prices refresh every day, in the market you choose.
