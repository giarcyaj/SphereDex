Built for collectors, not spreadsheets.

From your first Foxparks to the last chase card, SphereDex keeps the whole set organised, valued, and easy to complete.

Every card, every set

Every card across the Dawn of Palpagos booster, both trial decks, and the event promos, with new sets added as they release. Browse by set with real set art, track base cards and their variants, and watch your completion climb.

Scan, wishlist & graded

In the iPhone and Android apps, point your camera at a card and SphereDex recognises it, so adding cards takes seconds. Keep a wishlist, mark favourites, log graded slabs (PSA, BGS, CGC and more), add notes, run multiple collections, or import a CSV.

Power up your spheres

Completion is a game. Fill sets, chase parallels, and climb the sphere ranks as your binder grows toward 100 percent.

Real market values

Want the numbers too? See what your cards are actually worth: value your collection by the last 5 eBay sold prices or the current live listings, your choice, in your own currency, and watch the total move over time. Backed by eBay, Palworld Prices and CardTrader (EU asking prices).

Biggest movers

The day's biggest risers and fallers across your collection or the whole set, so you always know what is heating up and what is cooling off.

Sealed products

Track booster boxes, packs, and trial decks, follow preorders for upcoming sets, and jump straight to buy links from eBay, Amazon, and trusted regional stockists.

Know what it is worth, before you buy or trade.

Open any card for the real numbers: the average of its last five sold prices, the recent sales behind that figure with links to each listing, and the current live asks, in the market you choose. Live prices refresh every day, and sold prices refresh when you open the app or tap refresh. And they are completely optional: switch prices off any time for a clean collection with no numbers.
