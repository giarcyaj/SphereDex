Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

The short version

SphereDex is built privacy-first. Your collection is saved on your own device. We do not use ads, analytics, or trackers, and we never sell your data. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to create an account, use cloud sync, or contact us.

What is stored on your device

By default, everything SphereDex knows about you stays on your device (in your browser or app storage): the cards you own, wishlist and favourites, graded slabs, notes, sealed products, settings, and the market prices you have looked up. You can export this to a file, or clear it at any time by clearing the app or browser data.

Optional account and cloud sync

If you choose to create an account to sync your collection across devices, we store on our server:

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password, used only to sign you in.
  • Your collection data (the same information listed above), so it can sync between your devices.

This is entirely optional. If you never sign in, none of this is collected. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time using the contact details below.

Contact form

If you send feedback through the app, we receive the name, email address, and message you provide, so we can read it and reply. It is delivered to us by email through our email provider (Resend) and is used only to respond to you.

Camera

On supported apps, SphereDex can use your camera to scan a card number and add it to your collection. This happens entirely on your device using the device's built-in text recognition. Camera images are never uploaded, stored, or shared.

Prices and card data

To show market values, the app asks our server for prices using only a card identifier and your chosen currency or region. No personal information is sent. Prices are sourced from public marketplace data via Palworld Prices and eBay listings. Buy links open the retailer's own website, where that retailer's privacy policy applies.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or usage tracking, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for their own use.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you remove it (clear the app or browser storage, or export then delete). If you created an account, contact us to delete your account and all associated data from our server.

Children

SphereDex is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions, or want your account data deleted? Use the feedback option inside the app, or email us at craigjayedit@gmail.com.
