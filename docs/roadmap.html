Product status

Roadmap

A current snapshot of what is built, what still needs real-world acceptance, and what remains for later. “Built” describes implementation; it does not mean every platform release or device check is complete.

Updated 26 September 2026 · Based on repository code, published workflow runs, and recorded deployment/acceptance notes.

Built or shipped

Core product capabilities implemented; acceptance gates are separate below.

  • Action-led Home, configurable navigation, persistent Add/Scan, More menu, page-back history, shared completion calculations, Set Spheres and Missing Cards goals.
  • Deck builder and deck-to-collection/wishlist actions; onboarding; PalDex; wishlist, sealed products, releases/news, rewards, favourites and market views.
  • Scanner quick/review/bulk modes, capture correction and undo; separate raw editions; CSV/JSON export and import; Collection list and partial-playset filter; typo-tolerant local search.
  • Local “While You Were Away” / weekly summary; release and promo grouping; push preference/backend implementation; fail-soft operational digest.
  • Market source distinction, observation ledger, demand-aware bounded refresh and protected aggregate reporting are implemented/deployed; effectiveness still depends on production measurements.
  • PalDex search hint corrected. App source and web/Android/iOS outputs were published. Sieve uses Infisical, and scheduled refresh is twice daily at 08:00 and 20:00 UTC.
  • 26 September Sieve evidence: official scrape extracted 21 entries. No X entries were returned; current X request was refused under Conservative policy for X.com robots.txt Disallow: /.
  • Card reveals now come from the official site itself: a robots-permitted fetch of the official news API stages reveal posts (with cardlist art) into the Home feed — no scraping of X, and the reveal slide falls back gracefully until the first genuine reveal post.

Outstanding verification and release gates

Concrete acceptance and operational checks still open.

  • Sieve publishing fix in progress: delivered scrape files were saved beneath each session’s files/ directory, but the old consolidator only read root result.json. The X refusal also previously stopped the job before consolidation. The local fix now handles delivered JSON/CSV, isolates both scrape failures, and reports usable item counts. It still needs to be pushed and verified against a real run.
  • Complete web, Android and iOS acceptance for editions, scanner/batch review, collection list/filter, navigation, backup/deletion safety, onboarding and account migration.
  • Test APNs/FCM on real devices: permission denial, separate weekly/milestone/availability opt-ins, opt-out cleanup and notification-tap navigation.
  • Finalize Play/App Store privacy and data-safety answers against release binaries/dependencies, backend logs and retention configuration.
  • Accumulate production source observations and lookup outcomes; validate market reporting and allocation. Spain/Italy native-sold coverage is under target; keep spend/fallback unchanged until evidence gates pass.
  • Verify a scheduled (not just manual) refresh and budget actual Sieve credits per successful cycle.

Still to build or decide

Not claimed as delivered.

  • Deck-need push alerts, only if still desired and after explicit consent for the minimum server-side card/quantity data.
  • Privacy-safe public binders, visibility controls and read-only trade lists after P1 validation and consent/revocation review.
  • Advanced collector goals, deeper market intelligence and community/event additions after core flows and market data are validated.
  • Product analytics and scanner-correction uploads remain deliberately disabled pending separate opt-in, logging and retention approval.

Ideas to consider

Optional scope, guided by collector feedback.

  • Add a newly revealed card to Wishlist directly from News.
  • Show more useful price history and recent-sale ranges.
  • Compare sealed products with RRP and show release countdowns.
  • Explore trade and wishlist matching after enough active collectors are available.

Version history

Newest first.

v1.11
  • Notifications on iPhone are live: new sets, news, stock alerts and price alerts, the same as on Android
  • Honest prices: the last sold label now tells you how much of your market is a real sold price and how much is an estimate, and how many cards are more than two weeks old
  • New cards can reach the app without waiting for a store update, so a new set is there the day it is priced
  • The app tells you when a newer version is out
  • Prices keep trying: a refresh that fails while you are on a train or on hotel wifi retries by itself instead of leaving you on yesterday's numbers all day
  • The camera shows which collection your scans are going into, and remembers whether you left it on Code or Full card while you work through a stack
  • Your collection is safer: signing in on a second device now merges the two copies instead of letting either one overwrite the other, and sealed products, wishlist, favourites and decks all come through
  • A new install outside the United States now shows its own currency rather than dollars
  • Corrected release dates stay corrected, instead of quietly reverting once the announcement drops out of the news feed
  • Fewer blank screens: the app recovers by itself when the system shuts its page down in the background
v1.10
  • Price alerts: follow any card and get a notification when its price rises above or falls below a level you set, or moves up or down by a percent you choose. Live in the Android app now, and on iPhone as soon as iPhone notifications switch on
  • Sort your cards by recently added, plus a Recently added filter that fills the tick in blue on every card from your latest scanning or adding session, so you can see at a glance what you just added
  • Filter by set: choose Dawn of Palpagos, Legends Awaken, Eternal Ascent or Promo, then narrow to a product such as the booster box or one of the trial decks
  • A setting to show or hide the card number and rarity on card tiles, for a cleaner view of the art
  • A lighter app: card art now loads as separate images instead of being packed into the page, so the iPhone and Android apps use much less memory
  • Last sold prices now come from a new, more reliable source
v1.9
  • The scanner now handles graded slabs: it reads the grading label first, so a slabbed card is recognised without reading the card through the plastic, and the grading company, grade and cert number are filled in for you
  • Requested by a collector: export your collection as a spreadsheet (CSV) that opens in Numbers, Excel and Google Sheets, with every card, graded slab and sealed product priced in your currency. Import it back and slabs stay slabs
  • Export and import now work properly inside the iPhone and Android apps, with the share sheet on iPhone, Save as on Android and a file picker for imports
  • Scanned cards count towards your portfolio straight away: add a raw card or a graded slab from the scanner and its value joins that collection's total instantly, with slabs priced from recent sales at their company and grade
  • The scan popup shows how many of that card you already own, such as “You have 3 in Main · 1 graded” or “New to Main”, plus copies in your other collections and a ×N badge on each printing you own
  • All 275 cards now have built in art, including the last 20 promo cards, so every card shows its image offline in the iPhone and Android apps
  • Fixes and polish: card sheets open at the top, sign in fields suit the dark theme, Cards page selects are readable on phones, the collections menu stays on screen, Rarity key totals are correct, and there is a privacy policy link in the app
v1.8
  • A new card scanner: point your camera at a card and SphereDex recognises it by name, so adding cards is much faster. Choose a quick code scan or a full card scan
  • Fresher prices: live values now refresh every day and never get stuck, so your collection total always reflects the latest market
  • Last sold refreshes automatically when you open the app, shown by a loading ring on your portfolio graph
  • Four more grading companies to choose from: HGA, GMA, ACE and TAG
v1.7
  • Camera scanner, work in progress: point your camera at a card to add it. A first version that is still being refined, so recognition improves with each release as I try different approaches
v1.6
  • Stock alerts in the Android app (iPhone coming soon): turn them on for a tracked sealed product, such as a booster box, trial deck or sleeve set, and get a notification when it comes back in stock at a shop in your market. Covers shops in the UK, US and Australia, plus the two trial decks in Germany
  • Buy Sealed now spans more markets, with stockists across the UK, US, Australia and Germany
  • Buy buttons link straight to the exact product page instead of a search
  • Sealed product pages are centred and cleaner, and show the release date on every product

  • eBay results are now clean pills with the price, the date it posted or sold, and a link straight to the listing
