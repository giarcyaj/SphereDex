What I am building

Roadmap

What is shipped, what I am building now, and ideas I am weighing up. This is a living list and the order can change, but your feedback genuinely moves things up it.

Updated September 2026

Working on now

In progress, landing soon.

  • Tapping a rarity to open every card in that rarity
  • Biggest movers shown in your own currency and market
  • A clearer portfolio graph, with live and last sold shown separately
  • Tracking 1st versus 2nd edition per card, ready for the Dawn of Palpagos 2nd Edition reprint
  • Price alerts, so you can be notified when a card you follow moves
  • Japanese edition prices, switch any card between English and Japanese in your own currency

Planned

Coming, once the pieces are in place.

  • Per grade graded prices (PSA and others) once a graded sales feed is connected
  • New sets added as they release
  • Faster, smarter card scanning

Ideas I am considering

If interest is there. Tell me if you want these.

  • Sealed promo packs tracked as their own product, alongside the promo cards
  • Deck building
  • Trade and wishlist matching with other collectors
  • Better import and export

Version history

Newest first.

v1.3
  • Drag to reorder the menu, and it sticks
  • Promo cards with proper art and a showcase tile
  • A green or blue dot on card prices for live versus last sold
  • Read only graded price guide, tidier settings, and small polish
v1.2
  • Day by day portfolio history, switchable between live and sold
  • Sealed product pages with RRP, last sold, live listings and your value
  • Tournament and Masters League promo cards added
v1.1
  • Choose how your collection is valued: live listings or last sold
  • Value over time chart for your whole collection
  • Biggest movers, the day's risers and fallers
  • Sealed products priced right alongside your singles
  • Prices in your own currency and region
