## App Store – What's New in This Version

SphereDex 2.0 is our biggest update yet.

• A more useful Home: your collection, market value, next Set Sphere, closest set goal, decks and wishlist each get their own card with the next step to take.
• The headliner at the top of Home scrolls through the latest official news, posts from @PalworldOCG_EN, card reveals, and your biggest riser and faller side by side, with the headline beside the picture so it is always readable.
• PalDex: every printing of a Pal in one place, by Paldex number, with real Pal artwork.
• A floating menu along the bottom, with slots you can set to the pages you use most.
• A Palworld TCG news section, and new products, release dates, banners and set logos that arrive from the official news without waiting for an app update.
• Search ignores accents, so you find a card however you type its name.
• Importing a damaged backup no longer spoils your collection, and deck lists import with the right quantities.
• Easier with VoiceOver and a keyboard: every card, set and release can be opened directly, and pop-ups announce what they are.
• Stock alerts cover all eight markets again, and settings confirm when they have saved.
• Fixes: Set Sphere artwork on the Rewards shelf, the +/− buttons on 1st Edition cards, and undoing a scan now keeps a card on your wishlist if it was there before.
