## App Store – What's New in This Version

SphereDex 2.1 is for playing as well as collecting.

• The deck check now includes the 8 Lucky Pal limit. A card whose text lets you include any number of copies, such as Beegarde, Knight of the Flower Garden, is no longer flagged at four.
• Deck stats add a cost curve, counts for Pals, Events, Gear and Structures, a Lucky Pal count and the colour split.
• A match tracker for the table: both players, life from 10, Material and Ingredient, day or night, the round, and an optional 30-minute clock. It works offline.
• Open a card to read its rules text. Keyword chips explain Interrupt, Taunt, Nocturnal and the others. Search, filters and the deck builder can find a card by what it does.
• Decks, deck stats and the match tracker appear when you choose Play. Record what you paid when you choose Track value. Both can be switched in Settings.
• A Pal's page lists every printing of that Pal. Copies you already own stay on the list with a collection tick.
• Practice an opening hand on the deck page. The main deck is shuffled, five cards are dealt, and you may mulligan once by returning the whole hand. Pick a key card to see the chance of at least one copy in that hand, after one more draw, and after three more draws.
• Lucky Pal density sits with the Lucky count. Damage Check odds show the chance Strike 1, 2, 3 or 4 reveals a Lucky Pal and saves the life, using the full main deck as listed. A practice flip is optional.
• Copy for TCGplayer turns a deck's still-needed cards, the Wishlist, or a Missing Cards goal into the plain-text list Mass Entry accepts. Nothing is priced or fetched.
