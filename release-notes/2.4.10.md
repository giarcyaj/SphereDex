## App Store: What's New in This Version (English US)

SphereDex 2.4.10.

• Photographing a binder page on Android matches the cards again. Bundled card art had been blanking the match list.
• A card's printings follow the official rarity order. OSR sits straight after SR, before SP and SSP. The rarity sort, the rarity filters, the rarity breakdown, and previous and next inside a set use that same order. A saved collection is unchanged.
• Promo cards and Trial Deck base cards rank below Common. They no longer sort above chase cards. The key reads PR, TD, C, U, R, RR, then the variants.
• SP is named Super Parallel.
• TSR and TSP use their official names, Trial Deck Super Rare and Trial Deck Super Parallel. A spreadsheet exported before the rename still imports.
• On the rarity key, the base or variant badge sits beside the description, so a long name does not make one row taller. The value boxes line up at the bottom when a name wraps.
• The public site opens on a binder page of twelve real cards, drawn the way the app draws collected and missing cards.
• The What's new picture for 2.4.10 is in the app, with the three lines for this release.

Card names and art are used with credit to Pocketpair and Bushiroad. SphereDex is a fan made tracker and is not an official app.
