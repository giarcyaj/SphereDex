## App Store: What's New in This Version

SphereDex 2.4.5 redraws Home.

• Your collection is one square per card in the catalogue, lit for the ones you own and grouped by set.
• Your market draws its own value line. The figure counts sealed products as well as cards, so it matches the Market page. The tile can show everything you own, or cards on their own. Swipe between them on a phone, or use the arrows anywhere else.
• The next Set Sphere is a ring around the Sphere art.
• The cards you still need from a set, the ones on your wishlist, and the cards a deck is missing are shown as the cards themselves.
• Home lists the next release and up to three after it. A set that ships twice says which products each date is. The old single release line on Home is gone.
• Rewards adds a Statue of Power, earned by sharing the app. It is not earned by leaving a review. The Pal effigies now say what they are in SphereDex.
• Set tiles take their colour from the three cards on them, and the hard line across the top of those tiles is gone.
• A version check that fails asks again on the next wake. The update card names a release only once both stores have it.
• Sleeve & Card Set Vol. 1 is in the Android and iPhone scanners, including the Japanese print.
• A new set can reach people who have not updated, with its card art, once it is published.
• The second hand check is described as the rarest cards, which is where that panel appears. Add to calendar saves a calendar file for you to open. On a sealed product, price against RRP uses the cheapest shop that has it in stock when one does, and the market price otherwise.
