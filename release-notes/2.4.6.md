## App Store: What's New in This Version

SphereDex 2.4.6 completes Legends Awaken and adds Trades.

• Legends Awaken is complete: all 164 cards with official art, including the 30 SR and 16 OSR chase cards that the earlier partial list did not have. The set releases on 30 October 2026.
• Trades compares what you give with what you get, prices both sides, and keeps the history. The history syncs with your account and travels in your backup.
• Decks has a tournament check. Pick an event date and it says whether the deck is legal that day. English printings count toward the main deck, Soul cards keep every language, and a card whose English release comes after the event is marked not legal yet. A set with no exact release day is listed, never guessed.
• Decks keeps an event results log: the event, the date, the deck, each round, your placing and your prizes. A prize can be added to your collection, and the match tracker can log a result into an event.
• France, Germany and Italy name CardTrader as the European asking price source, beside eBay and Palworld Prices.
• A trade of a card you hold in more than one printing stops and points you to the card sheet, so it cannot take the wrong copy. Copies with no printing label still trade as before.
• A long trade history is no longer cut to 100 when two devices sync. Nothing is trimmed on sync. A new trade is kept up to 500, the same ceiling as the event log.
• A backup that holds trades and no cards restores. Before, the app refused it as unreadable.
• Tournament ready now counts the colour limit, four copies of a name, and the eight Lucky Pal cap. A deck that breaks those rules no longer gets a green headline above the red panel.
• The Not legal yet chip on a deck row wraps on a phone, so Decks no longer pans sideways.
