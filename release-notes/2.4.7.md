## App Store: What's New in This Version

SphereDex 2.4.7 shares a deck, photographs a page, and marks a Trial Deck as owned.

• Share a deck as a link. Anyone who opens it is asked whether to import it, and it arrives as a new deck without touching theirs. The deck is inside the link, so there is no server and no account. A hand edited link can only add cards the app already knows, and copies are capped at 60.
• Photograph a binder page or a spread and every card on it is read at once, on the device, then checked in the review list before anything is added.
• I own this deck, on each Trial Deck in Releases and on its sealed page. It shows every card in the box with how many copies the box holds, 50 cards and 10 Soul cards, then you pick the TSR or TSP that was in your box and the whole deck is added. Cards you already have are topped up to the box count, never counted twice. The two Trial Decks now say 60 cards, the 50 card deck and the 10 card Soul deck, instead of 40.
• Price a list. Paste a list of cards and get a total, with each one marked as new to you or already owned. Nothing is added to your collection, and a second box prices the other side of a trade against it. It is hidden when you have prices turned off.
• A printable checklist of the cards you are missing, grouped per set, with prices left out unless you include them.
• Filter cards by what a Pal is good at, on Cards, on the PalDex and in the deck builder. The PalDex count follows what that filter is showing.
• Add photos to a numbered copy or a graded slab. The pictures stay on that device and are never uploaded.
• Settings adds an App updates switch for the notice that a release has reached your store. It is on by default.
• The card scanner could not recognise any of the 109 Legends Awaken cards added when the set was completed, including every SR and OSR, because the list it matches against had not been regenerated. It has that list now.
• Searching an English card name with Japanese names switched on could miss the card when other sets had no Japanese text yet. A name match now counts as a name match.
• On Home, the grid of squares on Your collection was drawn over the card count and the percentage once Legends Awaken reached all 164 cards. It sits below that line now.
• Importing a spreadsheet with a Have column reads it as how many you own, and a blank there means none. Before, every row came in as one copy, so a list of missing cards could be added as owned.
• When an import cannot be used, for example the shopping list from Missing or Wishlist, the app explains why and where to export your collection from, instead of a message that vanished after two seconds.
• A sealed product page says when we could not reach the shops, instead of blaming the product, and shows the real recheck time of thirty minutes.
• On the web, a failed check for a new copy is no longer reported as an app error.
