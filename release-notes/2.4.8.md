## App Store: What's New in This Version

SphereDex 2.4.8 adds six ways to look after the cards you already have.

• Print your collection from Export. The report is grouped by set and includes serials, graded slabs and sealed product, with the date and a total. A second report leaves prices out. Open it and print, or save it as a PDF.
• Give an owned card a place name, such as Binder 2, page 5. Find my cards lists them grouped by place, and search finds the place as well as the card.
• Share your wants and your spares as a link, from the wishlist and from Extras. The person who opens it sees the list. Nothing is added to their collection. Prices stay off unless you choose to include them.
• Closest winning decks, on the Decks page, ranks official event decks by how close your collection is. Importing one makes a new deck and leaves the decks you already have alone.
• Mark a deck as Built so its copies stay with that deck. Other decks only count the copies that are still free, so two decks are not both ready when you only have the cards for one.
• Send a raw copy to be graded from the card sheet. It stays in your count while it is away. When it returns you log the grade and it becomes a slab, or you can bring it back with no grade.

Card names and art are used with credit to Pocketpair and Bushiroad. SphereDex is a fan made tracker and is not an official app.
