## App Store: What's New in This Version (English US)

SphereDex 2.4.9.

• Sixteen new English 2026 promo printings, EPR 021 to EPR 029: Relaxaurus Lux, Foxparks, Jolthog Cryst, the Frostallion Saddle, Dazemu, Seed Machine Gun, Mau, the Frostallion Noct Saddle, and Eikthyrdeer. The Pal cards and Seed Machine Gun also have an S printing. The two saddles do not. Rules text follows each card's Legends Awaken twin.
• Catalogue version 7 is newer than a stored catalogue of version 6, so an older install can receive these cards.
• The second hand check is at the bottom of the card sheet, after the place name, notes, and the set walk. The action buttons sit beside the price chart when the sheet is wide enough, and under the chart on a phone.
• The place name field is one line. It still saves the same trimmed 60 character value.
• The What's new picture for 2.4.8 is in the app.
• The release sweep reads sealed products from official product pages. Eternal Ascent trial deck posts for TD03 and TD04 can reach the stored trial deck rows. A street day already stored is left in place. A newer post that repeats that day keeps its clock, so an older post later in the same sweep leaves the day where it is. A date taken from a product page uses that page's own time, so a later recap leaves a delay notice in place. A failed catalogue read is not treated as an empty list, so linked products are not added as new rows. A news post is opened again for the product pages it links, even when the post text is unchanged.

Card names and art are used with credit to Pocketpair and Bushiroad. SphereDex is a fan made tracker and is not an official app.
