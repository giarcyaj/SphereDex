Rarity spheres

Each rarity powers up its own Pal Sphere as you complete it, Pal → Mega → Giga → Hyper → Ultra. Collect every card of a rarity to claim its Legendary Sphere.

App icon & theme

Fill up your master set to unlock these: Pal from the start, then Mega, Giga, Hyper, Ultra and Legendary at 20%, 40%, 60%, 80% and 100% collected. Set one as your app icon and colour theme, the header orb becomes that sphere and the app recolours to match. On the Android app your home-screen icon changes too (it may blink as Android reapplies it).

Quick add

Settings

Show prices & values
On: values, market prices and eBay listings appear next to your cards. Off: a clean binder with no prices anywhere.
Theme
Follow your device, or force light / dark. Remembered on this device.
Text size
Make everything in the app bigger or smaller. Remembered on this device.
Default card view
How the Collection grid looks by default.
Add cards to
When you tap “Collect” or a card’s binder button, add to this collection, or always ask which one.
Customise order of dropdown menu
The extra pages live in the ☰ menu at the top of the app. Use the arrows to set the order they appear in.
Don’t fade uncollected cards
Show every card in full colour, whether or not you own it, no dimming for missing cards.
eBay region
Which eBay marketplace the “Search sold listings” links open. Match it to where you buy and sell.
Fallback to US markets
If your region has no sold or listing data for a card, also check US eBay (ebay.com), more volume, but prices in USD. Off by default.
Default grading company
Shown as the default on the graded price guide and when adding graded slabs.
Refresh all prices
Update the market value of every card you own from the latest eBay sales.
Backup & restore
Export the active collection to a JSON file, import a backup, or import a spreadsheet (CSV) of card numbers and quantities as a new collection.
